Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored exclusively on your device using SwiftData. Nothing is sent to servers or third parties. If iCloud Backup is enabled, iOS may automatically back up app data to your iCloud account.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual shifts and jobs directly within the app. To delete AI Sync data from the configured server, clear the sync token in Settings. Šichta first asks the server to delete your synced jobs, shifts, and deletion records and revoke all sync tokens; the local token is removed only after the server confirms completion. If the request fails, the token remains visible so you can retry.

I lost data after an app update – what now?

Contact us with a description of what happened. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, data may be recoverable from your last backup via Settings → [Your name] → iCloud → Manage Backup.

The app isn't working correctly – what should I do?

Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, restart your device. Still happening? Email us with a description of the problem and your iOS version.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Šichta Pro includes CSV export. Open the History tab, tap the share icon in the top-right corner, and share the file with your accountant or save it to Files.
