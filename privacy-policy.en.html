2. What data do we collect, and where does it go?

All data you enter into the app — job name, hourly wage, shift times, tips, notes — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. The app contains no advertising networks and does not track you across apps or websites. Some data leaves your device in the four specific ways described below.

2.1 iCloud sync (always on)

šichta uses Apple's CloudKit private database to sync your data across your own devices when you're signed into iCloud. This sync happens directly between your device and your personal iCloud account; we do not have access to this data. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2.2 Currency exchange rates (always on, no personal data)

If you track a job in a foreign currency, the app fetches daily exchange rates from the Czech National Bank's public API (api.cnb.cz). This request sends only a date — no data that identifies you or describes your shifts, wages, or tips.

2.3 AI Sync (optional — off unless you turn it on)

Settings includes an optional "AI Sync" feature that lets you use an AI assistant (e.g. ChatGPT or Claude) to view and manage your shift data. This feature is off by default and only activates once you enter a sync token in Settings and tap "Sync now." When enabled, the app sends your job and shift data (job names, hourly wages, shift times, tips, notes) over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, authenticated with your sync token, to a server we operate. If you edit the server address in Settings, requests go instead to the address you configured, and that transfer is outside our control. Removing the token from Settings sends an authenticated revoke-and-delete request to the configured server: on a successful response, the server immediately deletes the synced jobs, shifts, and deletion records, revokes all sync tokens for that user, and only then does the app delete the local token. If the request fails, the app keeps the local token and shows an error instead of claiming that deletion completed; retry or contact us if the server remains unavailable.

2.4 Anonymous product analytics (on by default, can be turned off)

To understand whether setup is completed and which features are used, the app sends a small number of usage events to our own server — the same one described in 2.3, on a separate address that handles nothing else. There is no analytics company involved, no analytics SDK in the app, and no advertising network. This is switched on when you install the app. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy, and doing so discards the installation identifier described below and deletes anything still waiting to be sent, so nothing sent afterwards can be connected to what was sent before.

What is sent: event names from a fixed list (for example, which setup step you reached, or that a shift was added by hand rather than by timer), the app version and build, your iOS version to one decimal place, whether the device is a phone or a tablet, the app's language, and a random identifier generated on your device for this installation. That identifier is not your device's advertising identifier and not Apple's identifier for vendors — it is a random value created on your phone, and it is the only thing connecting one install's events to each other.

We do not send workplace names, wages, earnings, tips, payment amounts, contract type, notes, exact shift dates or times, calendar text, addresses, coordinates or geofence data. This is not only a promise: the server accepts only event names and property values from a fixed list and discards anything else before storing it, so a mistake in the app cannot result in us holding that data. Event times are rounded down to the minute, and your IP address is never recorded. These usage events are never combined with the shift data from 2.3, and the server has no way to tell which account, if any, an installation identifier belongs to. The data is stored in the EU, on hardware we run, and is deleted automatically after at most 400 days.

3. iCloud Device Backups (Apple)

Separately from the CloudKit sync described in 2.1, if you have iCloud device backup enabled on your device, iOS may automatically back up the app's local data to your iCloud account as part of your general device backup. This backup is entirely under your control and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to this data.

4. In-App Purchases

If the app offers paid features or subscriptions, all payment transactions are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store. We do not process or store any payment information. We only receive aggregated, non-identifying sales reports from Apple via App Store Connect. All transactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data, do not share it with advertisers, and use no third-party analytics provider. The usage data described in 2.4 stays on our own server. If you enable AI Sync (2.3), your job and shift data is sent to the server configured in Settings — by default a server we operate, used solely to let your connected AI assistant read and update your shift data. We do not sell or otherwise share that data with third parties.

6. Security

Your locally-stored data is protected by your iOS device protection (PIN, Face ID, Touch ID) — we recommend using a strong screen lock. If you use AI Sync, your data is also protected by your sync token: keep it private. Removing the token from Settings requests immediate server-side deletion and revocation on the configured server; the app does not remove the local token until that server confirms completion.

7. Your Rights under GDPR

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. For data stored only on your device or synced via your own iCloud account (2.1), these rights can be exercised directly on your device — data is deleted by uninstalling the app or via in-app controls. If you have used AI Sync (2.3), removing the sync token requests immediate deletion of synced data and revocation of all sync tokens on the configured server. The default server does not retain a separate backup or retention copy of AI Sync data. If deletion cannot be completed, the token remains in the app and you can contact us at the address in Section 11.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:
