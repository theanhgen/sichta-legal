Questions.

Is šichta a time clock or payroll system?

No. It is a personal income log for one person. Your employer can’t see it and approves nothing in it.

Where is my data?

On your phone, with a backup in your iCloud. If you turn on AI Sync, shifts are also sent to šichta’s server. The app sends anonymous usage statistics to its own server, with no ad networks, and you can turn them off in Settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does šichta do taxes?

It estimates net pay for DPP, DPČ and full-time (HPP) jobs in the Czech Republic. An estimate, not a payslip.

How much does it cost?

The basics are free; history and the calendar show the current month and the three before it. Pro unlocks your full history and CSV and PDF export. We’ll announce the Pro price before the App Store release.

Which languages?

Czech and English. Vietnamese, Ukrainian and Russian are in beta.

Where do I get it?

For now as a beta on TestFlight. The App Store comes later.
